COMMUNITY RULES

KAI'S HUB · COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

THE RULES.

Every player is expected to follow these rules in-game and in the community spaces.

  1. 01

    Respect Everyone

    Treat players and staff respectfully. Harassment, hate speech, and discrimination are not allowed.

  2. 02

    No Cheating

    Do not use unauthorized hacks, cheats, or exploits that give an unfair advantage.

  3. 03

    No Spam

    Do not flood chat, repeatedly post unwanted messages, or disrupt conversations.

  4. 04

    No Unauthorized Griefing

    Respect other players' builds and property. Follow the server's specific land and PvP rules.

  5. 05

    Keep Chat Appropriate

    Keep conversations suitable for the community. Do not post threatening or abusive content.

  6. 06

    Protect Privacy

    Never publish another person's private information or share private messages without permission.

  7. 07

    Follow Staff Instructions

    Cooperate with moderators. If you disagree with a decision, use the official appeal process.

  8. 08

    Report Problems Honestly

    Report rule violations and bugs through the official support channels. Do not make false reports.

  9. 09

    Use the Correct Channels

    Follow the rules of each Discord channel and the instructions posted by community staff.

Rule violations may result in warnings, temporary restrictions, or bans depending on severity. Staff should apply the server's published moderation policy consistently.
